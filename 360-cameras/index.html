GoPro Max: Connecting and Using

Connect the camera to GoPro Quik before using your phone as a remote control or transferring a photo.

Connect the GoPro Max to your phone

GoPro Quik
  1. Download and open GoPro Quik. You may select Continue as a guest.
  2. Select GoPro in the bottom-right corner, then choose Connect a GoPro and Max.
  3. Turn on the camera with the power button on its side. Do not use the red shutter button on top.
  4. On the camera screen, swipe down and select Preferences > Connect Device > GoPro App.
  5. Return to the phone app and select Continue. When the camera appears, select Connect Camera.
  6. Allow Bluetooth access and keep the camera’s default name.

Set the camera to take a 360 photo

Camera setup
  1. Press the power/mode button on the side of the camera until it is in photo mode.
  2. Check the mode icon in the lower-left corner of the camera screen.
  3. If the icon shows HERO mode, tap it and change it to the globe-shaped 360 mode icon.
  4. Position the camera, move yourself out of the main scene when possible, and take the photo with the camera shutter or the app.
Transfer the photo

In GoPro Quik, use View Media, select the image, and save it to your phone. You may then use AirDrop. You can also remove the microSD card and connect it to a computer with an adapter.

Ricoh Theta V: Connecting and Using

Register the camera in the Ricoh Theta app, join its Wi-Fi connection, and confirm that the app is set to still photography.

Connect the Ricoh Theta V to your phone

Ricoh Theta app
  1. Download the app named Ricoh Theta. Do not download similarly named apps such as Ricoh Theta m15 or Ricoh Theta+.
  2. Press and hold the camera’s side power button for about two seconds. A blue light should appear.
  3. Press the Wi-Fi button below the power button.
  4. Open the app and select the Shooting tab.
  5. When asked to select a camera, choose Other Models.
  6. Use the phone camera to scan the QR code on the bottom of the Theta.
  7. Select Register, leave Use the default password selected, and choose Join when prompted.

Take a still photo with the Theta app

Shooting controls

Make sure the still-photo camera icon is selected at the top of the shooting screen. You can press the shutter button in the app or use the physical shutter button on the camera.

Exposure adjustment

Brighten or darken the photograph before shooting.

Shooting modes

Choose Auto, Shutter Priority, ISO Priority, or Manual.

Shooting options

Access Noise Reduction, DR Compensation, HDR Rendering, and Handheld HDR.

Preview mode

Switch among a movable 360 view, no preview, and an equirectangular preview.

Transfer files

Open Cam Images in the app to download photographs from the connected camera. By default, transferred images should also be saved to your phone’s photos or media library.

On a Mac, you may instead connect the Theta by cable, open Image Capture, select the camera, and save the chosen images.

Viewing 360 Photos

A 360 photograph must be opened in a compatible viewer to become an image you can pan around. In a standard image viewer, it may appear as a wide, distorted rectangle.

View a photo on your phone

Mobile
  1. Open the camera’s app and navigate to its media or image area.
  2. Select the downloaded 360 photograph.
  3. Drag across the image to look in different directions. Depending on the app, you may also be able to move the phone to change the view.

View a photo with the desktop Ricoh Theta viewer

Desktop
  1. Locate the 360 image file on your computer.
  2. Right-click or Control-click the file.
  3. Select Open With, then choose Ricoh Theta.
  4. Drag inside the viewer to look around the scene.
